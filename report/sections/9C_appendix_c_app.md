# Appendix C The deployed application

The application at https://mlda-cw1-8han4medy3o7d8izzcywbm.streamlit.app serves the committed artefacts of the study through seven pages. {fig:app1}, {fig:app2}, {fig:app3}, {fig:app4}, {fig:app5}, {fig:app6} and {fig:app7} were captured from the live deployment on 21 October 2026 at a 1,400-pixel viewport, after the feature-timing audit and the rerun of 20 October, so the numbers they show are those of Sections 2 to 5: the Evaluation page in {fig:app6} shows a test PR-AUC of 0.842 and, at the capacity threshold of 0.211, a recall of 0.941 and a specificity of 0.406, and the Preprocessing page in {fig:app3} counts the 52 input columns.

The Inference page shows, for each report, the class at the capacity threshold, which applies to all filings, beside the workload-rule decision for open reports at the model's pre-fixed cut-off (0.376 for HGB), after asking whether a return-to-work date is already on the form; a batch upload receives both flags. It validates MINE_ID: an entry of 1 to 7 digits is padded with leading zeros to the 7-digit identifier, any other entry is refused with an error and nothing is scored, and a well-formed identifier unknown to the reference data produces a warning and is scored with the mine context entered by hand. The Evaluation page has a Capacity policy tab with the workload rule and the calibration on open reports, and the About page states the revised decision rule. Every page imports the same `mlda` package as the notebook and loads its numbers from the artefacts on the main branch, from which the deployment is built. The rerun was committed there as 2695aca ({tab:commits}), so the application draws on the same artefacts as Sections 2 to 5. The Preprocessing and Model training pages refit on samples of the training rows only, and the Inference page scores a new report through the saved pipeline.
